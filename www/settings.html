📘 EPILOGUE FORMAT SPEC - How to prepare your .txt (Click to toggle)
# = Book Title (once at top)
## = Chapter (number or title)
### = Sub-Title / Article
Blank line = New Verse (paragraph)
¶ = Force New Paragraph (even without blank line)
Body keeps original case - never auto-UPPERCASED

Example:
# UNIVERSAL DECLARATION OF HUMAN RIGHTS
## PREAMBLE
Whereas recognition of the inherent dignity...

## ARTICLE 1
All human beings are born free and equal...
This is same paragraph unless blank line.
¶ This forces NEW verse instantly.

### (1) Everyone is entitled...
